# Acme Studio billing and refund policy

## Team seats and invoices

Additional team seats are prorated from the day they are added through the end of the current billing cycle. Owners can review itemized charges in Settings → Billing → Invoices. Explain the billing dates and additional seats using the customer's invoice; do not guess amounts that have not been verified.

## Refund eligibility

Customers can request a refund within 14 days of their initial purchase. The maximum refundable amount is the unrefunded balance of the original successful payment. Partial refunds use the payment's original currency. A workspace owner must approve the exact amount and payment identifier before a refund is issued.

## SupportPilot test environment

SupportPilot only issues Stripe test-mode refunds. An owner must explicitly link a Stripe test payment to the selected ticket. Real customer payments and live API credentials are not supported in this pilot. When eligibility is uncertain, ask an owner to review the case rather than promising a refund.
