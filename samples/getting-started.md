# Getting started with Acme Studio

## Your first workspace

Create a project, invite your teammates, and add your product documentation. Choose a clear workspace name that your team recognizes. Workspace owners manage integrations and permissions; agents work on support tasks and drafts.

## A connected support workflow

Connect Gmail to read messages from a selected support label. Connect Slack to propose escalations to your approved team channel. Import selected Google Drive documentation into your shared workspace knowledge after confirming that the entire team may search the snapshot.

## Thoughtful customer replies

Start by acknowledging the customer's question. Give concrete steps supported by the current product documentation. Avoid promises, invented policy, and requests for secrets. End with a useful next step. Every outbound reply must be reviewed by a workspace owner.
